Design guidelineRound 3

Quiet surfaces, one accent, motion that explains.

A portfolio that reads in five seconds, scans in a minute, and still feels like it was built by someone who ships. This is the rulebook the pages that follow are built from.

02Principles

Six rules every decision answers to

One accent
Teal marks what is alive or actionable: the active nav item, the role line, progress, status. Everything else stays neutral.
Proof before prose
Numbers, logos and projects come before paragraphs. A visitor should grasp the stack without reading a sentence.
Motion explains
Animation shows state (live, loading, progress) or rewards attention (hover). It never delays content.
Nothing hides behind hover
Every action is visible and tappable on a phone. Hover only adds polish.
Calm density
Lines under 62 characters, 16 to 17 px body, 120 px between sections, one idea per card.
Accessible by default
AA contrast, real buttons and links, visible focus, one h1, reduced motion respected.

Reduced motion: entrances become 150 ms fades, loops stop, the marquee holds still and wraps.
Hire me sits in the nav and again in the contact band. Resume buttons live in the hero.
The nav highlights the section in view and anchors clear the fixed bar.
Every action is visible without hover. Project cards keep View Details on screen.
Email has a copy button. Form fields have labels, inline errors and a success toast.
Projects have three states: skeleton while loading, a friendly empty state, and an error with Retry.
One h1 (the name), one h2 per section. Icons carry labels. Focus ring is 2 px brand.
Touch targets are at least 44 px. On phones, buttons stack full width and chips wrap.
Weight stays light: only the first project image is prioritised, two font families.
